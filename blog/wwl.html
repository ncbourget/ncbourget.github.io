WHITE, WHITE LIE

I live in new york city now. I just graduated college for graphic design with honors (massive subtle flex), i spent the summer in allston ma, and now i’m here in the joke nightmare of bushwick bklyn.

I get bogged i get bogged okay? Everybody is always up in my fucking ear like ‘you should be enjoying it more!’...”Its new york for god sake.” which makes it impossible to complain and -whats more?- is absolutely humiliating when i speak about myself. Bushwick is full of this slop and slime, you see wiseguys online making fun of the people that live here all the time, its all you see if you’re on instagram as a vulgar and somewhat angry 20 something artist. These people and the giant septums, the carefully crafted black ink tattoo sleeves. everything has already been thought and said and spat about this fucking slum. And yet i decided to move here deliberately after virtually scorching the earth back home. And here is the humiliating part: *deep breath* I am a wayward digital artist with no head on his shoulders to speakof. And I think all the time. And All i do is think, and my thoughts bring me to my knees and my knees bring me to my tears. My thoughts pollute my mind, in the amygdala lies a factory creating enough product and pollution as a baogang. I am a wayward digital artist. The only reason I am out here is for work. I am here on a mission for work. Cut and Dry Cut&Dry graphic fucking design! But its all dried up. Now all I do is the same few freelancing projects. Now all I do is stress and complain

New challenge I came up with but I'll say that I saw online: how many days in a row can you stay in your apartment without leaving? this is only a quarter of my negativity right now. I have just now figured out that my closest friends in college have had false preconceptions of me that my ex-girlfriend was spreading. People very close to me, close friends knew about this and still did not tell me so i had to find out and stumble into it myself. A baldfaced lie, that I had cheated on her. And though that is something I never did I find the true crime that none of my fuuuucking day ones stepped forward. My ex girlfriend who I never had any ill will towards, did not have a dramatic breakup, parading around with a juche i would never imagine myself to replicate. Now there are people i know committing suicide, many more developing true mania. I am finding myself in the midst of the bipolar generation. Negativity? Yes I am negative right now. Negativity? How could you not be? No job, no prospects, no acolytes. I feel like sometimes I'm one bad day away from the same. Sometimes one bad moment.
